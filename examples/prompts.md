# Example prompts

Prompts that work well with the le4ds MCP. The assistant always creates drafts first and asks before scheduling.

## Getting started

> List my le4ds accounts and tell me which formats each one accepts.

> What do I have scheduled for next week?

## One video, several networks

> Upload `~/Videos/launch-9x16.mp4`. Create a post for Instagram and Facebook as a Reel, and for TikTok,
> with a short caption for each network. Plan it for Tuesday at 9:00 for Instagram and Facebook and 18:00 for
> TikTok, Lisbon time. Show me the plan before scheduling.

## A week of posts

> Here are five captions for next week (below). Create one draft per day, Monday to Friday, on LinkedIn and
> Facebook, with the images in `~/Pictures/week-42/`. Don't schedule anything yet.

> Schedule the five drafts you just created at 10:00 Lisbon time, each on its day.

## LinkedIn carousel

> Upload `carousel.pdf` and create a LinkedIn carousel post with this text: "…". Plan it for Thursday 8:30.

## Fixing problems

> Did anything fail this week? For each failed post, tell me the error and what I should change.

> Move tomorrow's 9:00 posts to 8:00.

> Cancel the TikTok post scheduled for Friday.

## Agencies (Vault add-on)

> List my Vault clients, then show the accounts for "Café Central".

> Create a draft for Café Central's Instagram with this photo and caption: "…".
